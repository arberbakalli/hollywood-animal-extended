SECTION 1: STUDIO POLICIES (THE 4 CORE PATHS)
Hegemon (Expansion - Recommended for Empire Runs):
Pros: Unlocks elite post-production studios; passive commercial buff helps films retain viewers longer.
Cons/Traps: Requires buying up competitor theaters to sustain multi-film slates. AI competitors buy theaters aggressively (per the simulation update), requiring careful Alliance manipulation.
Factory (Assembly Line):
Pros: Focuses on predictability, steady profits, and assembly-line output. Generates consistent genre films without relying heavily on high-tier artistic talent. Grants passive story element unlocks.
Boutique (Artistic / Achievement Runs):
Pros: Unlocks Breakthrough Masterclasses (breaks skill ceilings). Required for the Level 10 Wendy Carpenter achievement.
Cons/Traps: Heavily reliant on Pollux awards. Pollux voting involves heavy RNG and high influence costs.
Trashking (Grindhouse):
Pros: Trivializes early cash flow by slotting "trash tags" into low-budget double features.
Cons/Traps: Caps long-term artistic progression and limits high-end prestige playstyles.
